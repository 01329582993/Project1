01.
The Networking Problem
  • High 3D UAV mobility (10 m/s) causes rapid wireless link degradation.
  • Greedy geographic forwarding gets trapped in routing voids.
  • Standard Q-routing explores blindly, triggering massive latency spikes (>90 ms).
02.
The RLFR Solution
  • Bounded Latency: Enforces a strict 40 ms QoS delay budget via a Risk Table.
  • Energy Efficiency: Selects dynamic relay power levels (25, 50, 75, 100 mW).
  • Cooperative Mesh: Shares state values in beacons for fast convergence without central servers.
2

The Three Core Pillars of RLFR

1. Multi-Objective Utility

Rather than optimizing purely for distance or delay, RLFR formulates a joint utility combining delivery success, packet latency, and transmission power.

u = κ − c1τ − c2w
2. Safe Latency Exploration

Maintains an explicit Risk Table R(s, a). In the Boltzmann action policy, choices that historically breach the 40 ms latency deadline are strongly penalized.

π(s,a) ∝ exp(Q − c·R)
3. Distributed Bellman Updates

Drones piggyback their local state-value V(s) into periodic 0.5 s HELLO beacons. Neighbors learn cooperatively with zero central coordination.

Q ← (1−α)Q + α[u + λmax Q + υΣVj]

Loop Elimination: Cache Ω

Every drone maintains a cache Ω of (source_id, packet_id) tuples. If a packet loops back due to mobile topology shifts, it is dropped instantly.

Result: Zero broadcast storms and elimination of routing cycles.

Mathematical Formulation: Utility & Power Control

Multi-Objective Utility (Equation 3)
u(k) = κ − c1·τ − c2·w

Quantifies the multi-hop transmission reward when forwarding data packet k.

Parameter Value Physical Meaning
Delivery Success (κ) 1 or 0 Confirmed via destination ACK
Normalized Latency (τ) c1 = 0.8 End-to-end packet delay / Tmax
Transmission Energy (w) c2 = 0.6 w = p · (z / r) (Power × Duration)
Relay Power Action Space
a = (next_hop, p),   p ∈ {25, 50, 75, 100} mW

By choosing power dynamically based on link SNR ξ, drones conserve battery and shrink the spatial interference footprint.

The Single-Objective Failure

Greedy forwarding picks maximum-distance hops with high bit error rates. RLFR balances distance against transmit energy and delay penalties.

5

Mathematical Formulation: Safe Exploration & Risk

Safe Boltzmann Policy (Equation 2)
π(s, a) = exp(Q(s,a) − c·R(s,a)) / Σ exp(...)

The risk weight c = 0.5 penalizes actions that violate latency constraints.

Risk Table Update (Equations 5 & 6)
l(k) = 1(τ > μ)   [μ = 40 ms threshold]
R(s,a) ← (1 − β)·R(s,a) + β·l(k)

β = 0.8 enables rapid exponential smoothing to detect fading channels instantly.

Why Safe Exploration is Essential
  • No Blind Exploration: Unlike ε-greedy, RLFR never explores links that risk breaching the 40 ms deadline.
  • Real-Time Adaptation: If a drone moves behind an obstacle, its R(s,a) jumps to 1.0, and the policy immediately switches to an alternate path.
  • Guaranteed QoS: Mission-critical video and telemetry stay within delay tolerances.
6

Distributed Bellman Update with Shared Experience

Distributed Bellman Equation (Equation 4)
Q(s,a) ← (1 − α)·Q(s,a) + α [ u + λ·max Q(s′, â) + υ·Σ Vj(sj) ]

Hyperparameters: Learning rate α = 0.70, Discount factor λ = 0.90, Shared experience weight υ = 0.05.

Beacon Value Piggybacking

Every UAV calculates its local value function Vj(sj) = max Q(sj, a) and embeds it into periodic 0.5 s HELLO beacons.

Zero extra protocol messages; fully decentralized.

Fast Cooperative Convergence

Instead of waiting for ACKs from the destination, a relay immediately learns from its neighbors' evaluated state values via υ·Σ Vj.

Code Implementation Architecture in UavNetSim

Package: routing/rlfr/
  • rlfr_packet.py:
      • RLFRHelloPacket: Beacons coordinates, battery, and V(s).
      • RLFRAckPacket: Carries κ, delay τ, and link SNR ξ.
  • rlfr_table.py:
      • Implements Q-table, Risk-table R(s,a), and loop cache Ω.
      • Computes safe Boltzmann exploration policy.
  • rlfr.py:
      • Protocol controller: packet queuing, CSMA/CA scheduling, and Bellman updates.
Simulator Integration
  • entities/drone.py: Registered in protocol factory map.
  • routing/parameters.py: Configured paper hyperparameters.
  • main.py CLI: Executable via --routing RLFR.
  • 3D Web UI: Interactive selection at localhost:8000.
Clean Interoperability

Shares the exact same SimPy environment, CSMA/CA MAC, and physical channel models as the other 9 protocols for zero-bias benchmarking.
